About AI-ABSA: The Incremental Sentiment Engine

Hello! This is a website we built focusing on analyzing the emotional aspects of AI. This project was chosen by our group as a report for our Natural Language Processing course. Basically, we will be researching and building AI models to predict the emotional aspects of a quartet using data from r/LocalLlama, a very popular site for sharing AI models.

01
Notes

The training data was entirely created by us, so it's a bit limited, which means the model's responses aren't always perfect or accurate. Please bear with us, hihi!
